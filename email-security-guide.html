Why email security is important for companies

Protect a business-critical channel

Email carries invoices, customer requests, and operational instructions. A misleading message can disrupt work or trigger an incorrect payment.

Combine controls and habits

Review account access, use multifactor authentication, and establish a separate verification step for changes to payment instructions. Technology works best alongside clear procedures.

Make review manageable

Create a consistent way to report suspicious messages and review quarantined items. Track trends without turning every alert into an interruption.
