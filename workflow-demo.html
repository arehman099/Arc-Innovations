SAMPLE SOLUTION CONCEPT

Email & approval automation

Requests are copied between inboxes and spreadsheets.

This is an illustrative design, not a deployed client case study.

THE APPROACH

A practical starting point.

A proposed workflow captures requests, prepares a draft, and routes approval.

  1. Capture request details and validate required fields.
  2. Prepare a draft response and route it to a person for approval.
  3. Record the approved action and make retries safe.

Before and after

Before: manual searching or repeated request handling. Proposed after: a guided workflow with human review and a traceable record.

Time savings and error reduction must be measured in a pilot against an agreed baseline.
