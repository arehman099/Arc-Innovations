How Power BI dashboards help small businesses

Start with a decision, not a chart

Choose a question your team asks regularly: which products drive revenue, where costs are rising, or which orders need attention. Define a small set of consistent metrics before designing a dashboard.

Connect and check the data

Sales exports, stock records, and finance data often use different labels. Agree the definitions, reconcile totals, and set a refresh schedule suited to the source systems.

Keep the first version focused

Use a readable overview, relevant filters, and clear definitions. Review the dashboard with the people making decisions and measure whether reporting becomes easier.
