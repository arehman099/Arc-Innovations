5 ways AI can automate business operations

Document intake

Extract structured fields from recurring documents and validate them before updating a business system.

Company knowledge search

Retrieve approved source material and produce answers with references. Access control must apply before retrieval.

Email triage

Classify incoming requests, propose a response, and retain human approval for consequential messages.

Reporting summaries

Draft commentary from validated metrics. Check that every figure can be traced to its source.

Demand forecasting

Use historical data to explore expected demand, then compare forecasts with simple baselines before relying on them.
