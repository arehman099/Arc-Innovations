Email security checklist

Use these prompts to review your current process with the person responsible for your IT.

  • Review multifactor authentication and privileged account access.
  • Review procedures for reporting suspicious messages.
  • Verify payment-detail changes using a known independent contact channel.
  • Check domain authentication and mail-filter configuration with your administrator.
  • Review quarantine access and release approval responsibilities.
  • Review account recovery, backups, and incident response contacts.
  • Keep staff guidance current and test the reporting process.
