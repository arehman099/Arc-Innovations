Enquiries

The form asks for your name, email, service, and message; company and phone are optional. In the default email mode, the website prepares a draft in your email application. It does not store the form contents on a website server. Information is sent only when you send that email. We use the received information to respond and discuss a potential engagement, including steps requested before entering a contract. Please do not include sensitive personal data.

External contact channels

Opening a WhatsApp link takes you to an external service. Its provider’s privacy terms apply there. Your email provider also handles an email you choose to send.

Hosting and optional analytics

The site is intended for GitHub Pages hosting, whose provider may process technical request information under its own terms. Optional Google Analytics is disabled in the supplied configuration. If configured, it loads only after you accept analytics. See the Cookie Policy for controls. Search Console ownership verification does not itself add visitor tracking.

Retention and recipients

Received enquiries are retained only as necessary to respond, manage follow-up, and meet applicable obligations. Authorised team members and the providers used for email and hosting may process information within their roles. We do not use enquiry details for unsolicited marketing through this form.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability where applicable, and object to processing where that right applies. Optional analytics choices can be withdrawn using Cookie preferences. Contact info@arcinnovations.it to exercise your rights. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
