Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

Default website behavior

The supplied website uses local fonts and local images. Background videos are replaced by static visuals. No optional analytics or advertising scripts load in the default configuration.

Preference storage

When you choose analytics preferences, the website stores that choice in local storage under arc-analytics-consent-v1. This value contains only granted or denied, not enquiry details. It remains until you change it or clear your browser storage.

Optional Google Analytics

If the site owner enables a Google Analytics measurement ID, the website asks for consent before loading the analytics script. Google Analytics may use _ga and related _ga_* cookies to distinguish visits, typically for up to two years depending on property settings. It may process usage and device data outside Italy. Enquiry field values are not sent to analytics by this site.

Change your choice

You can reject optional analytics, accept them, or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal reloads the page with analytics disabled and clears accessible Google Analytics cookies set for this domain. Essential hosting logs are outside this control.
